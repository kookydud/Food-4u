# Food 4U — Video Script: "Food that fits you"

**Length:** about 11:26 · **Rendered video:** `C:\Projects\Ionic\food4u-video\Food4U_Walkthrough.mp4`

**The message:** most delivery apps show everyone the same menu. Food 4U learns how you eat (Personal Taste) and shapes the whole app around it: meals picked for you, allergy and avoid warnings on every menu, and a meal screen that starts with your spice level and cooking preferences.

**Demo profile used:** Halal-friendly · allergic to Egg + shrimp (custom) · avoids Garlic · Less oil · Spicy.

Demo code for phone and payment verification: **123456**.

---

## 0:00 · Title card — *Food 4U: Food that fits you*

## 0:05 – 0:36 · Disclaimer — Before we begin

**ON SCREEN:** Disclaimer card with these points:

- This demo shows the front end of Food 4U, a working prototype.
- It shows what users can expect when they use the app.
- Accounts, payments, orders and delivery tracking are simulated on the device.
- Restaurants, riders and payment providers are not connected yet.

**SAY:**
> Before we begin, a quick note. What you're about to see is the front end of Food 4U, a working prototype of the app. It's here to show what users can expect when they use it. Accounts, payments, orders, and delivery tracking are all simulated on the device for this demonstration, and the app isn't connected to real restaurants, riders, or payment providers yet.

## 0:36 – 1:21 · Welcome to Food 4U

**ON SCREEN:** Login screen.

**SAY:**
> Hi! This is Food 4U, a food delivery app built with Ionic and Angular. Most delivery apps show every customer the exact same menu, whether you're vegetarian, allergic to shellfish, or just can't stand garlic. Food 4U is different. It learns how you eat, and then shapes the whole app around you. In the next ten minutes, I'll show you how that works, from setting up your taste profile to ordering a meal that fits you. Let's start from the very beginning, as a brand new user.

## 1:21 – 1:44 · Sign up

**ON SCREEN:** Tap **Sign Up**, fill in name, email, password twice, tap **Create account**.

**SAY:**
> It starts with a quick sign up. We just need a full name, an email address, and a password of at least six characters, typed twice so there are no typos. The password is hashed before it's saved, so it's never stored as plain text.

## 1:44 – 2:10 · Phone verification

**ON SCREEN:** Enter a phone number, **Send Code**, type **123456**, **Verify code** → Home.

**SAY:**
> Next, we verify a phone number. Pick your country code, enter your number, and tap Send Code. For this demo, the code is always one, two, three, four, five, six. Once it checks out, we land on the home screen.

## 2:10 – 2:49 · A first look at Home

**ON SCREEN:** Point at the address and bell, scroll the restaurants, then the purple **Make Food 4U yours** card. Tap it.

**SAY:**
> This is Home. Up top is your delivery address, and the notification bell. Below are our nine partner restaurants, with photos, ratings, delivery times, and fees. But notice this purple card: Make Food 4U yours. Right now the app doesn't know anything about us, so everyone sees the same thing. Let's change that. We could tap into any restaurant now, but the menus would look the same for everyone.

## 2:49 – 3:45 · Personal Taste · ⭐ personalization

**ON SCREEN:** Personal Taste: **Halal-friendly** → allergen **Egg** → add custom allergy **shrimp** → avoid **Garlic** → **Less oil** → spice **Spicy** → **Save preferences** (show the confirmation).

**SAY:**
> This is Personal Taste, the heart of Food 4U. First, food preferences. I eat halal, so I'll pick halal friendly. Next, allergens. I'm allergic to egg, and I can also add my own allergy that isn't on the list, like shrimp. Then, ingredients I'd rather avoid. Garlic, for me. Cooking preferences: I like my food with less oil. And finally, spice level. I love spicy food. Every choice is optional, and you can change it whenever you like. Save. From now on, every screen in the app uses this profile.

## 3:45 – 4:22 · Picked for you · ⭐ personalization

**ON SCREEN:** Home now shows **Picked for you**. Swipe through the row, point at a green reason line, tap the top pick (Chef's Special Curry).

**SAY:**
> Back on Home, that purple card has become Picked for you. These meals were chosen from every restaurant for this profile. They're all halal friendly, spicy dishes come first, and anything with egg, shrimp, or garlic has been left out completely. Each one even says why it was picked. Change your profile, and this list changes with it. A plain menu can't do that. Let's tap the top pick.

## 4:22 – 5:05 · Allergy alerts on every menu · ⭐ personalization

**ON SCREEN:** Open **Green Wok Kitchen**: red *Allergy alert: shrimp* (yakisoba) and *egg* (katsu curry), green *Fits your diet* (tofu rice). Tap **+ Add** on the yakisoba to show the warning on the meal screen.

**SAY:**
> Now let's visit a restaurant we haven't been picked into. Green Wok Kitchen. Straight away, the menu warns us. The garlic shrimp yakisoba gets a red allergy alert for shrimp, and the chicken katsu curry is flagged for egg. Meanwhile, the teriyaki tofu rice gets a green tag: it fits your diet. If I open the yakisoba anyway, the meal screen repeats the warning, before anything goes into the cart. This works for the built-in allergens, and for anything you type in yourself.

## 5:05 – 5:38 · Ingredients you avoid · ⭐ personalization

**ON SCREEN:** Open **Mediterranean Bowl**: amber *Contains garlic* on the shawarma, green tags on falafel and couscous. Show **Reviews** and **Info** tabs.

**SAY:**
> Allergies are red, but things you simply don't like are marked in amber. At Mediterranean Bowl, the shawarma comes with garlic yogurt, so it's marked: contains garlic. The falafel and the couscous fit my diet, so they're tagged in green. Every restaurant page also has reviews from other customers, and an info tab with the address, opening hours, and contact number.

## 5:38 – 6:08 · Browse & search

**ON SCREEN:** Home: tap **Pizza**, **Burgers**, **Offers**. Search tab: type *ramen*. Offers tab.

**SAY:**
> You can still browse everything, of course. Category pills filter the restaurants instantly, like pizza or burgers. And search looks through restaurant names, cuisines, and dishes as you type, while remembering your recent searches. And there's an Offers tab, with deals like buy one get one, and half price favorites.

## 6:08 – 6:41 · A meal that starts personalized · ⭐ personalization

**ON SCREEN:** Spice Route → **+ Add** on Chef's Special Curry. Point at **Spicy** (pre-selected) and the note **Less oil** (pre-filled). Choose **Large**, quantity **2**, **Add to Cart**.

**SAY:**
> Let's order that top pick, the Chef's Special Curry from Spice Route. Look at the customize screen. Spicy is already selected, because that's my spice level. And the note to the kitchen already says, less oil, straight from my cooking preferences. I didn't type any of that. I'll go for a large, make it two, and add to cart.

## 6:41 – 7:14 · Cart, checkout & vouchers

**ON SCREEN:** **Go to Cart** → **Checkout** → **Choose voucher** → **SAVE25** → show the discount.

**SAY:**
> The cart keeps everything, including that note for the kitchen. At checkout, we see our items, our delivery address, and a note for the rider. Food 4U supports vouchers. Some, like SAVE25, work at every restaurant, and others belong to one place. The app checks the voucher against this order, and shows the discount before you pay.

## 7:14 – 7:39 · Payment options

**ON SCREEN:** Select **GCash** → Place Order (show the GCash form) → back → **Cash on Delivery** → **Place Order**.

**SAY:**
> There are three ways to pay. Cash on delivery, GCash, or a credit or debit card. GCash and card payments open a payment form, followed by a verification screen. For this demo, I'll keep it simple with cash on delivery. Place order!

## 7:39 – 8:31 · Order tracking

**ON SCREEN:** Track Order: status + countdown, map with the rider moving, timeline, rider card, order summary.

**SAY:**
> After placing the order, we can follow it live. At the top is the current status, and a countdown to arrival. The map shows the route from the restaurant to our address, and once the rider picks up the food, you can watch them move along it. The timeline lists every step, from order placed to delivered, each with the time it happened. You can see your rider's name, rating, and plate number, with buttons to call or chat. And the order summary keeps the items, your kitchen note, the payment method, and the address together. And it keeps updating, even if you close the app and come back later.

## 8:31 – 8:53 · Notifications

**ON SCREEN:** Home → bell badge → Notifications → **Mark all read**.

**SAY:**
> Every status change also sends a notification. Back on Home, the bell shows a red badge. Tap it to see all your updates, newest first. Tap one to jump back to that order, or mark everything as read.

## 8:53 – 9:17 · Orders

**ON SCREEN:** Orders tab: current order with status + **Track**; scroll to past orders and **Order Again**.

**SAY:**
> The Orders tab lists current orders, each with its status and a Track button. You can cancel while the kitchen is still preparing, but once the rider has your food, cancel disappears. Below that is your history, where Order Again rebuilds a past order in your cart with one tap.

## 9:17 – 9:49 · Delivered & rate your order

**ON SCREEN:** Track page shows *Delivered*. Rate **5 stars**, comment, **Post review**, open the restaurant's **Reviews** tab.

**SAY:**
> And our curry has arrived. The timeline is complete, and the order has moved into our history. Food 4U then asks how the food was. Five stars, and a quick comment. Post. And here it is on the restaurant's reviews tab, helping the next customer decide. Reviews you post can be edited or deleted later, too.

## 9:49 – 10:26 · Account

**ON SCREEN:** Account tab: profile card, **Personal taste** at the top of Perks, then **Address**.

**SAY:**
> In the Account tab, your profile shows your name, email, how many orders are on their way, and whether you have an active cart. Personal Taste sits right at the top of your perks, so you can update it any time, and the whole app adapts again. In Address, you can save several places, and choose which one is primary. Your saved addresses are used at checkout, so you never have to type them twice.

## 10:26 – 10:50 · Vouchers, Pro & friends

**ON SCREEN:** **Vouchers** → **Food4U Pro** → **Invite friends** → **Help center**.

**SAY:**
> You'll also find your vouchers, Food 4U Pro, a membership with monthly and quarterly plans for member only discounts and priority support, and a page to invite friends with your own code. And if you ever need a hand, there's a help center, plus our terms and policies.

## 10:50 – 11:21 · Food that fits you

**ON SCREEN:** Home: swipe the **Picked for you** row once more, scroll the restaurants.

**SAY:**
> So that's Food 4U. One taste profile, and the whole app changes. Meals picked for you, warnings before you order something you can't eat, and a kitchen note that writes itself. Everything is saved on your device, so it's all still there next time. Food 4U: food that fits you. Thanks for watching!

## 11:21 · End card — Thanks for watching!
